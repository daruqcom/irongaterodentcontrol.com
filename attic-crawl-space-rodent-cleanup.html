How it works

Steps for Effective Rodent Cleanup

We start with a comprehensive inspection of your attic and crawl space to identify signs of rodent activity, including droppings and nesting materials. This step typically takes about an hour.

Our team sets up rodent traps and bait stations to capture any existing rodents. This process usually takes 1-2 days, depending on the severity of the infestation.

After removal, we thoroughly clean the affected areas using EPA-approved cleaning supplies. This step ensures that all droppings and contaminants are safely disposed of.

We identify and seal any entry points to prevent future infestations. This step is crucial and typically takes 1-2 hours, depending on the layout of your home.

A follow-up inspection is conducted 2-4 weeks later to ensure the effectiveness of our cleanup and prevention methods, ensuring your home remains rodent-free.
