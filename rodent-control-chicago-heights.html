How Seasons Affect Rodent Control in Chicago Heights

Chicago Heights experiences seasonal changes that impact rodent behavior, making it important to adjust control strategies throughout the year.

❊

Spring

As temperatures rise, rodents become more active, seeking food and nesting sites. Homeowners should be vigilant during this season.

Tip Schedule a spring inspection to identify and seal entry points before infestations begin.

☀

Summer

Warm weather leads to increased outdoor activity, which can drive rodents indoors in search of cooler environments.

Tip Maintain cleanliness around your property to deter rodents and consider professional inspections.

❄

Rodents prepare for winter by seeking shelter in homes, making this a critical time for inspections and preventative measures.

Tip Ensure your property is sealed and inspect for signs of rodent activity before winter sets in.

❅

Winter

Tip Schedule winter inspections to address potential rodent problems before they escalate.
