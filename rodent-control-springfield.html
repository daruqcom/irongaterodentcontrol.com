How Seasons Affect Rodent Control in Springfield

Springfield's climate significantly influences rodent behavior and control needs throughout the year. Understanding these seasonal patterns can help you stay ahead of infestations.

❊

Spring

As temperatures rise, rodents become more active, searching for food and nesting sites. This leads to increased sightings in homes.

Tip Schedule a spring inspection to identify and seal potential entry points before infestations occur.

☀

Summer

Warm weather encourages rodents to breed, leading to higher populations. Outdoor areas may also attract them.

Tip Maintain cleanliness in outdoor spaces to deter rodents and consider regular inspections.

❄

Tip Prepare your home by sealing entry points and scheduling a pre-winter inspection.
